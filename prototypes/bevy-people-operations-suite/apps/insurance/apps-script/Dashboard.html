How the numbers work

Claims at every stage appear here. Missing settlement and bank dates show as Pending. Only settled claims with a checked Bank Received box and valid bank receipt date count as completed. Averages use completed payments only. Completed claims are filtered by bank receipt date; other claims use their start, received or settlement date. Rejected claims are filtered by their start date, and only the count is shown.

Rejected includes insurer rejections and HR’s automatic rule: more than 30 working days from the start date without a Bank Received tick. This automatic classification does not confirm insurer rejection. Start means HR approval unless an actual submission date is recorded. Working days exclude Friday and Saturday. Public holidays are not excluded. Same-day steps count as 0 days. Averages use the completed claims in your selected dates, before rounding. Counts represent claims, not people.
